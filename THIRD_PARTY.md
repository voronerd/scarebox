# Third-party components

scarebox's own code, configs, docs, line library, voice reference clip and sound effects are
Apache-2.0 (see `LICENSE`). Everything below belongs to someone else and keeps its own licence.

Almost none of it is in this repository. Models, voices, Python packages and container images are
downloaded or built on your own machine (`scripts/stage_models.sh`, `deploy/Dockerfile.app`,
`deploy/Dockerfile.chatterbox`, `deploy/images.env`). The **Shipped here?** column says which few
pieces this repo does carry.

Every licence below was checked against its upstream page or package index entry on 2026-10-25
(Hugging Face model cards, PyPI metadata, the NVIDIA catalog, Debian copyright files). Licences can
change between releases: if you pin a different version, check again. The project's rule is that
the code, models and voices it chooses are Apache-2.0, MIT, BSD or CC0 (ADR 0001 §1d); the
**Notes** column flags the handful of copyleft or proprietary pieces that arrive inside the
container images, and why they are there.

## Excluded on licence

Considered and left out because their licences don't allow what this project needed (details and
dates in `docs/MODELS.md`): Ultralytics YOLO (AGPL-3.0), Coqui XTTS-v2 (CPML, non-commercial),
F5-TTS (CC-BY-NC-4.0), Spotify pedalboard (GPL-3.0; the voice effects are hand-written in numpy and
scipy instead), and current Piper TTS releases (`piper-tts>=1.3.0`, GPL-3.0-or-later).
