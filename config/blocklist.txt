# config/blocklist.txt -- deterministic censor layer (03-01).

# One term or short phrase per line. Matched with word boundaries against
# normalise(text) (see scarebox/censor.py): lowercased, leetspeak-folded,
# separators stripped, 3+ repeated letters collapsed to 1. Blank lines and
# lines starting with # are ignored. Costume-specific exceptions (e.g. "baby
# shark", "dead pirate") are NOT here -- they live in censor.py's ALLOWLIST
# and are checked before this file, gated by the runner's costume category.

# "gender" and "disability" classes deliberately do NOT list everyday nouns
# (boy/girl/man/woman/wheelchair) -- those appear constantly in benign lines
# ("young lady", "little monster") and blocking them would strangle every
# legitimate joke. Only mockery-coded terms are listed for those two classes.
# The guard model (Task 2) is the backstop for anything that slips past this
# narrower deterministic net.
